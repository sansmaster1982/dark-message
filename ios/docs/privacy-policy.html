Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

Overview

Dark Message Crypto Chat ("the App") is a fully offline encryption application. Your privacy is our top priority.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. The App operates entirely offline and does not connect to the internet. No personal information, usage data, analytics, or telemetry is collected, stored, or transmitted.

Encryption

All encryption and decryption operations are performed locally on your device. Your messages, photos, documents, and passwords never leave your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising frameworks.

Data Storage

All data (chat metadata, encrypted content, app settings) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes all associated data.

Photos

The App requests access to your photo library solely to allow you to select photos for encryption and to save decrypted photos. Photos are processed locally and are never uploaded or shared.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
